- Usage guidelines

## General Printing Guidelines

### Recommended Settings
- **Layer Height**: 0.2mm (standard) or 0.12mm (high quality)
- **Infill**: 20-30% for most applications
- **Supports**: Model-dependent (check individual documentation)
- **Adhesion**: Brim or raft when needed for bed adhesion

### Material Recommendations
- **PLA**: Easy to print, good for indoor use
- **PETG**: Water-resistant, ideal for wet environments
- **ABS**: High temperature resistance, requires enclosure
- **TPU**: Flexible parts, requires slower printing

## Customization

Most models are created in OpenSCAD and can be easily customized by modifying the parameters at the top of each `.scad` file. Common adjustable parameters include:
- Dimensions and sizes
- Wall thickness
- Tolerances and clearances
- Resolution settings
